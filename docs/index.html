    How colours are assigned
    1. The model predicts a disease probability for every day of 5 years of NASA POWER climate for Ranchi.
    2. Probabilities are averaged for each calendar date across years and smoothed with a 7-day window.
    3. Days are ranked within the year: bottom 40% Low, 40–70% Moderate, 70–90% High, top 10% Very High. The calculator uses the same probability cut-offs: .
    Terms & Conditions

    Disease incidence is subject to rearing season, crop stage, and management practices. This calendar indicates climate-based risk only and should be used as a decision-support tool, not a definitive forecast.

    About this calendar

    Each cell is one day of a typical year. The colour shows the disease probability predicted by the Random Forest model, ranked across the year.

    Inputs: daily maximum and minimum temperature, relative humidity, THI and wind speed.

    Limitations: the model was trained on a small dataset (116 days) collected mainly during the rearing season, so risk levels are relative (which parts of the year look most disease-favourable), not absolute outbreak probabilities.
